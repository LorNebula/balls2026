Ship  
Every ship has 4 main components: reactor, thrusters, shields and weapons. Every ship has a different amount of components they can have, but the starter ship has 1 of each. These components are physically shown on the ship so they can be targeted by weapon systems.

You can open the ship menu with a command, and it shows a zoomed in version of the ship. You can see all the components and see information like health, shield, and power.

Each component has their own amont of health.  
Damaging the reactor would lower the ship's power. Destroying the reactor would make the ship powerless.  
Damaging the shields make the ship generate less shield. Destroying the shields make the ship have no shield.  
Damaging the weapons make the weapons take time more to charge up. Destroying the weapons render them useless.  
Thrusters do not do anything right now.

The ship has a power bar which increases over time depending on the reactor. Power has a maximum amount which is also dependant on the reactor. These get decreased if the reactor is damaged.

Every component (except the reactor) uses a set amount of power. Shields, turret recharge and turret firing. If there is not enough power they cannot function.
