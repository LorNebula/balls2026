Galaxy
The galaxy is randomly generated with a seed. This galaxy will stay the same until it has been told to regenerate.
The galaxy would have an unexplorable black hole in the center. Around the blackhole in a spiral shape form like a galaxy there will be star systems, connected to eachother by hyperlanes.
The star systems should be named based off of Finnish city names, and other spacial objects (like black holes and nebulas) should be named after Finnish river or lake names.
Each star system can have 0-3 planets out of these types:
- small, medium, large size
- gas, terra, oceanic, volcanic type
- has zero or one moons. these moons can be tiny or small
In the galaxy there are also nebulas, which affect 1-5 star systems near it. They do not do anything yet, just show to the player that the star system is in a nebula.
Each star system may also have asteroids that act as enemy ships. These asteroids can be mined with weapons and they give loot.
Each star system may have a space station with a market. In these star systems there are no enemy or asteroid encounters.

Market
The market can be opened using a command. In the market you can sell cargo and buy other cargo or components to upgrade your ship with. Each market has their own inventory and prices. Show the market using a table.

Moving
You can only move to a star system adjacent to the one you are currently in, and is connected by a hyperlane. Each move costs 1 fuel.
